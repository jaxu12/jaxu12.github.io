Privacy Policy

Infograf

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Jan Freda, operating as Jachsu Games ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information in connection with Infograf (the "App"), our picture-and-fact puzzle game for all ages, including children.

The App stores puzzle progress on your device. Ad-supported releases use Google AdMob, and a one-time purchase through Google Play can remove advertisements. The advertising and purchase sections below apply when these features are available in your version of the App. Development or offline versions without these integrations do not send information to these services through the App.

Google Play Games is a possible future feature and is not currently enabled. Section 7 explains its proposed scope. We will update this Policy to describe the actual integration before enabling it.

2. Data Controller and Contact

Controller: Jan Freda, operating as Jachsu Games

Country of operation: Poland

App: Infograf

Privacy and support requests: contact.jachsugames@gmail.com

Parents and legal guardians may use the same contact address for questions or requests concerning a child's information.

3. Information Handled by the App

  • Local game data: completed puzzles, unlocked gallery pictures, unfinished puzzles and their piece positions, the side of each puzzle currently shown, and preferences such as the selected appearance theme. This information is saved on your device and is not uploaded to us.
  • Advertising service data: when ads are enabled, Google AdMob may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device and App information, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and permitted identifiers. Advertising is subject to the child-protective restrictions in Section 4; the Android advertising identifier is not sent under this configuration.
  • Purchase information: when purchases are enabled, the App processes the product identifier, purchase token, purchase status, and the entitlement to remove ads as needed to recognize and restore the purchase. Google processes payment information; we do not receive full payment-card details.
  • Support correspondence: if you or a parent contact us, we receive the sender's email address and information included in the message. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information about a child.

The App does not require a Jachsu Games account or ask you to provide your name, email address, or date of birth to play. It does not request access to contacts, the microphone, the camera, or precise device location.

We have not added Google Analytics for Firebase, custom gameplay telemetry, or a developer-operated player database to Infograf. Advertising providers may still process service-related measurement and diagnostics as described above.

4. Advertising and Children's Privacy

Infograf is intended for children and adults. In ad-supported releases, we apply child-protective advertising settings to all users, without asking children to identify themselves or collecting their birth dates.

Ads are configured without interest-based personalization or remarketing, with child-directed treatment and an age-appropriate content limit. We use advertising SDK versions eligible for the Google Play Families program. This configuration prevents sending the Android advertising identifier (AAID); the App does not send SIM or device serial numbers, IMEI, IMSI, or Wi-Fi network identifiers for advertising.

Non-personalized advertising can still involve limited processing, such as IP addresses, ad interactions, and diagnostics, for delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. It does not mean that no information is processed.

We do not knowingly collect children's direct contact information through gameplay or use their information for behavioral advertising. If a parent or guardian believes that information about a child was collected improperly, they can contact us to request review and deletion of information under our control.

5. Advertising Privacy Choices

Where applicable, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides advertising privacy notices and choices. Available choices depend on your region and the child-protective configuration. A consent choice does not enable personalized advertising in Infograf.

When Google requires an option to revisit advertising privacy choices, ad-supported releases provide it in the App's settings. You may also use applicable Android and Google account privacy controls. If the required privacy conditions for an ad request are not met, the App does not request the ad and you can continue playing.

Where consent is the legal basis, you may withdraw it using the available privacy controls or contact us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew consent. Buying ad removal is optional and is separate from any privacy choice.

6. One-Time Purchase to Remove Ads

When offered, Remove ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed through Google Play Billing. It removes all in-game advertisements while the purchase entitlement is valid. It is not a subscription.

Google handles the payment through your Google Play account. The App uses purchase information to recognize the entitlement, acknowledge the purchase, restore it on the appropriate Google Play account, and handle changes such as refunds. An entitlement may be cached on your device so that ad removal continues to work offline.

Removing ads stops future in-game ad requests once the entitlement is recognized. It does not delete information already processed by Google, remove Google Play's purchase history, or stop the purchase checks needed to restore and maintain the entitlement.

Purchases by children should be managed by a parent or guardian through the available Google Play purchase approval and authentication controls.

7. Possible Future Google Play Games Features

Google Play Games Services is not currently integrated, and Infograf does not currently send player profiles, achievements, or cloud saves to that service.

A future version may offer optional Google Play Games features such as achievements or cloud saves. Depending on the features actually added, Google and the App could process a player identifier, display name, avatar, sign-in status, achievements, and saved puzzle progress. Google may also process service diagnostics and usage information. We would not receive your Google password or full payment-card details.

Before enabling these features, we will update this Policy with the exact data, purposes, retention, deletion controls, and service providers involved. Availability for children and supported accounts will be determined under Google's requirements and applicable law. Access to the core puzzle game will not require Google Play Games sign-in.

8. Service Providers and Sharing

For the features described above, information may be handled by:

  • Google AdMob and UMP: ad delivery, privacy choices, service measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention in ad-supported versions.
  • Google Play Billing: payment processing, purchase recognition, acknowledgement, and restoration when purchases are available.
  • Email services: delivery and storage of correspondence when you contact our Gmail support address.

Google Play Games is not an active provider for Infograf at this time. We do not currently upload your puzzle progress to a developer-operated server.

Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy and its information about apps using Google services.

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We may disclose information under our control where required by law or necessary to protect legal rights and security.

We handle information to save and restore local progress, deliver permitted ads, recognize and restore ad removal, respond to support requests, and protect transactions and services.

Where the GDPR, UK GDPR, or similar law applies, the relevant legal bases may include:

  • Performance of a contract: providing requested game features and delivering or restoring a purchase.
  • Consent: accessing or storing advertising information on a device where consent is required. Non-personalized advertising does not remove applicable consent requirements. Where parental authorization is required, we must obtain it or avoid the processing that requires it.
  • Legitimate interests: responding to support requests and protecting against errors, security threats, or fraud, where applicable and after considering your rights, with particular attention to children.
  • Legal obligations: complying with applicable transaction, recordkeeping, and other legal requirements.

10. Retention

  • Local saves, settings, and cached entitlements: remain on your device until cleared in system settings or removed with the App, subject to operating-system backup and restoration behavior.
  • Support correspondence: kept only for as long as needed to handle the request, resolve related issues, and meet applicable legal obligations.
  • Google service data: retained by Google according to the applicable service settings, retention rules, and legal requirements. We do not set or promise a single retention period for Google's records.

Deleting local game data does not delete your Google Play purchase history or Google's advertising records. The App does not currently keep developer-managed cloud saves or player accounts.

11. Deletion and Your Privacy Rights

You can remove local Infograf saves and settings by clearing the App's storage through Android settings or uninstalling the App. This removes puzzle progress on that device. A valid ad-removal purchase can be recognized again through the Google Play account used to buy it.

For information we hold, including support correspondence, contact contact.jachsugames@gmail.com to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or portability where applicable. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights, including U.S. state privacy rights. A parent or legal guardian may make a request concerning a child.

We may ask for limited information needed to verify a request. We will not ask for your Google password. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes; if so, we will explain the applicable limitation.

For information controlled by Google, use Google account data and privacy controls or Google's relevant service controls. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority; in Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).
