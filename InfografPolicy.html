Infograf

Last updated: October 18, 2026

1. About This Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Jan Freda, operating as Jachsu Games ("we", "us", or "our"), handles information in connection with Infograf (the "App"), our picture-and-fact puzzle game intended for users aged 13 and over.

The App stores puzzle progress on your device. Ad-supported releases use Google AdMob, and a one-time purchase through Google Play can remove advertisements. The advertising and purchase sections below apply when these features are available in your version of the App. Development or offline versions without these integrations do not send information to these services through the App.

Supported versions initialize Google Play Games Services for platform authentication. Progress synchronization is a separate optional feature, as described in Section 7. The full game remains available without a Google Play Games account or cloud save.

3. Information Handled by the App

  • Local game data: completed puzzles, unlocked gallery pictures, unfinished puzzles and their piece positions, the side of each puzzle currently shown, and preferences such as the selected appearance theme. This information is saved on your device. If you enable cloud save, the puzzle progress described in Section 7 is also saved through Google Play Games; it is not sent to a developer-operated server.
  • Advertising service data: when ads are enabled, Google AdMob may process your IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device and App information, ad interactions, diagnostic information, and permitted identifiers. Advertising is subject to the child-protective restrictions in Section 4; the Android advertising identifier is not sent under this configuration.
  • Purchase information: when purchases are enabled, the App processes the product identifier, purchase token, purchase status, and the entitlement to remove ads as needed to recognize and restore the purchase. Google processes payment information; we do not receive full payment-card details.
  • Support correspondence: if you or a parent contact us, we receive the sender's email address and information included in the message. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information about a child.

The App does not require a Jachsu Games account or ask you to provide your name, email address, or date of birth to play. It does not request access to contacts, the microphone, the camera, or precise device location.

We have not added Google Analytics for Firebase, custom gameplay telemetry, or a developer-operated player database to Infograf. Advertising providers may still process service-related measurement and diagnostics as described above.

4. Advertising and Children's Privacy

Infograf is intended for users aged 13 and over, not directed to children under 13. We apply the same child-protective advertising settings to all users, including teenagers. The App does not ask for or store your age group or date of birth, and does not enable personalized advertising for older users.

Ads are configured without interest-based personalization or remarketing, with child-directed treatment and an age-appropriate content limit. We use advertising SDK versions eligible for the Google Play Families program. This configuration prevents sending the Android advertising identifier (AAID); the App does not send SIM or device serial numbers, IMEI, IMSI, or Wi-Fi network identifiers for advertising.

Non-personalized advertising can still involve limited processing, such as IP addresses, ad interactions, and diagnostics, for delivery, measurement, security, and fraud prevention. It does not mean that no information is processed.

We do not knowingly collect children's direct contact information through gameplay or use their information for behavioral advertising. If a parent or guardian believes that information about a child was collected improperly, they can contact us to request review and deletion of information under our control.

5. Advertising Privacy Choices

Where applicable, Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) provides advertising privacy notices and choices. Available choices depend on your region and the child-protective configuration. A consent choice does not enable personalized advertising in Infograf.

When Google requires an option to revisit advertising privacy choices, ad-supported releases provide it in the App's settings. You may also use applicable Android and Google account privacy controls. If the required privacy conditions for an ad request are not met, the App does not request the ad and you can continue playing.

Where consent is the legal basis, you may withdraw it using the available privacy controls or contact us. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew consent. Buying ad removal is optional and is separate from any privacy choice.

6. One-Time Purchase to Remove Ads

When offered, Remove ads is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed through Google Play Billing. It removes all in-game advertisements while the purchase entitlement is valid. It is not a subscription.

Google handles the payment through your Google Play account. The App uses purchase information to recognize the entitlement, acknowledge the purchase, restore it on the appropriate Google Play account, and handle changes such as refunds. An entitlement may be cached on your device so that ad removal continues to work offline.

Removing ads stops future in-game ad requests once the entitlement is recognized. It does not delete information already processed by Google, remove Google Play's purchase history, or stop the purchase checks needed to restore and maintain the entitlement.

Purchases by children should be managed by a parent or guardian through the available Google Play purchase approval and authentication controls.

7. Google Play Games and Optional Progress Sync

Cloud save is optional. After the tutorial, you can choose Connect to Google Play Games (or Turn on progress sync if already signed in) or Not now. You can also connect later from Settings → Google Play Games. The full puzzle game and local save remain available without a Google Play Games profile, without signing in to Play Games, and offline. Purchases and purchase restoration use Google Play Billing independently of Play Games.

The App initializes the official Google Play Games Services SDK and checks platform authentication independently of progress sync. Google may sign in an existing eligible Play Games profile automatically and process profile, device, diagnostic and service information. Creating a missing profile is deferred until you choose Connect. Turning off progress sync does not stop platform authentication or sign you out. After you turn on sync, the player identifier associates local progress with the correct cloud account. Before sync is enabled, the App does not read or upload your puzzle snapshot. The App does not request your Google password, read your email, or use your player identifier for advertising.

The cloud copy contains completed card identifiers and difficulty levels, unlocked sets, tutorial completion, and unfinished puzzles with their grids, piece arrangements, displayed sides and resume order. Saved Games metadata includes a generated preview of the puzzle arrangement, a progress description, and accumulated foreground puzzle play time. The preview uses only game artwork and pieces; it contains no account name, email, purchase information, or screenshot of other apps. It excludes purchase tokens, the ad-removal entitlement, advertising privacy choices, advertising counters, and device preferences such as audio volume. Google Play Billing separately restores ad removal.

Google stores the cloud copy for your Play Games account. Synchronization runs after you enable it, groups ongoing changes at intervals of at least two minutes, and refreshes when you return to the App. Uploading your current progress can happen during play; importing changes from another device waits for a safe menu. Selecting the Infograf saved game in Google Play Games requests restoration through the same protected flow. If progress sync is off, the App asks you to turn it on in the game before accessing the snapshot; an external launch cannot override your sync choice. Offline changes remain in the local save and are retried. If devices disagree about a puzzle, the App offers a choice before replacing its arrangement. Account changes require a choice before copying local progress to another account.

You can stop future cloud-save requests with Turn off progress sync. This does not sign you out of Google Play Games. This leaves your local progress and the existing cloud copy intact. Delete saved progress removes the Infograf cloud snapshot for the connected account and turns off synchronization; your local progress remains. Other devices with synchronization still enabled may create a new cloud copy, so turn off cloud save on those devices as well if you want it to stay deleted. Google also provides Play Games and Google account controls for removing game data.

The App keeps account-association information, its last synchronized copy, and local recovery backups on your device. These are used to prevent account mixing and recover from interrupted synchronization. Clearing App storage removes these local files. Google may process profile, device, diagnostic and service information under its privacy policy. We do not operate a player database or add Firebase Analytics.

The App is intended for users aged 13 and over. Creating a Play Games profile and syncing progress are optional and subject to Google's account eligibility and parental controls. We do not collect dates of birth or bypass Google's account restrictions. A parent or guardian can use the contacts and controls in this Policy.
