Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 31 March 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Jachsu Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the mobile game CosmIDLE: Idle Space Shooter (the "App"). The App is designed so that users can play without creating a developer-managed account and without providing personal data directly to us. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect and process certain information as part of their functionality.

3. Data Collection Overview

We distinguish between:

  • Data collected directly by us: We do not require users to provide personal data to use the App, and we do not operate our own account system.
  • Data collected by third-party services: The App integrates third-party services (such as Google AdMob, Google Play Billing, and Google Play Games Services) that may collect and process data. We do not use data collected through these services to personally identify users.

4. Data Collected by the Developer

We do not directly collect personal data through normal use of the App. We may only receive data in limited situations:

  • Support communication: If you contact us via email, we will receive your email address and any information you choose to include.
  • Local data storage: The App may store gameplay progress and settings locally on your device. This data is not transmitted to us.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)
  • Google Play Games Services (optional features)

Review the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Google AdMob (Advertising)

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process information to display ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and provide personalized ads.

Ads may be personalized based on your interests, depending on your location and consent settings.

This may include:

  • Advertising identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • Device information (e.g., device model, OS version)
  • Network-related information (such as IP address)
  • Interaction data (e.g., ad views and clicks)

7. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

  • All transactions are processed by Google.
  • We do not have access to your payment details (such as credit card information).
  • Payment information is stored and handled by your Google account.

8. Google Play Games Services

The App may include optional features such as achievements, leaderboards, and cloud saves. If you choose to use these, Google may process your player profile, gameplay progress, and scores.

9. Automatically Collected Data

Third-party services may automatically collect technical data, including device information, identifiers (Advertising ID), network-related data, and diagnostic performance data.

10. How Data Is Used

Data processed by third-party services is used to provide ads, process purchases, enable game features, and prevent fraud. We do not build user profiles ourselves.

Users in certain regions (such as the European Economic Area) may be presented with a consent request regarding personalized advertising in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

We do not track users across apps or websites ourselves. Any such tracking is performed by third-party services such as Google AdMob in accordance with their policies.

11. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may disclose information if required by law.

Third-party services may process data as independent controllers in accordance with their own privacy policies.

12. Data Retention

  • We do not maintain a database of user personal data.
  • Local game data can be removed by uninstalling the App.
  • Third-party services retain data according to their own policies.

13. User Rights and Choices

You can reset or disable ad personalization in your Android settings and manage your Play Games settings via your Google account. You can stop all data collection by uninstalling the App.

Since we do not store personal data, we are unable to process data deletion requests. Users can manage or delete their data through third-party services or by uninstalling the App.

14. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Children should use the App only with parental consent if required by law.

If required, we rely on Google Play's age-based settings and protections for advertising and data processing.

15. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is completely secure.
