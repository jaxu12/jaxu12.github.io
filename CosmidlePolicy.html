1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Jachsu Games ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use CosmIDLE: Idle Space Shooter (the "App"). You can play without creating a developer-managed account or giving us your name or email address. The App nevertheless uses optional analytics, advertising, purchasing, and platform services described below.

You may use the email address above for privacy questions and requests. A postal address for a legally required request can be provided through the same contact channel where applicable.

3. Information We Process

Depending on the features and choices you use, we or our service providers may process:

  • Optional analytics and telemetry: a random installation identifier and random session identifier; event times and timestamps; screens visited and time spent using the App; game progress, upgrades, prestige, quests, achievements, and sectors; interactions with the shop and advertisements; and platform, language, App version, and release channel.
  • Advertising data: Google AdMob may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device and operating-system information, approximate location inferred from network information, and ad impressions, clicks, rewards, and related interactions.
  • Purchase data: Google Play Billing processes transactions and may provide us with product, purchase status, and transaction-validation information. We do not receive full payment-card details.
  • Play Games data: if you choose to sign in, Google Play Games Services processes your Google Play Games profile and sign-in status. The current App integration does not upload cloud saves and does not use Play Games leaderboards or Play Games achievements.
  • Support data: if you contact us, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message.

Our custom telemetry does not send your email address, player name, Google Advertising ID, or Android ID. Its random installation identifier is generated by the App and is not a Google account identifier.

4. Optional Analytics and Custom Telemetry

The App may use Google Analytics for Firebase and our own limited telemetry delivered through Google Cloud Functions and stored in Cloud Firestore. Analytics is voluntary, disabled by default, and starts only after you make a separate affirmative choice.

We use analytics to:

  • measure engagement, session length, and play time;
  • improve game balance and the tutorial;
  • understand player progress and gameplay funnels;
  • detect errors, abnormal activity, and potential abuse; and
  • measure advertising and shop-feature performance.

We do not use our custom telemetry to identify you by name or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Separate Analytics and Advertising Choices

The App presents two independent privacy choices:

  • Anonymous analytics consent: controls Google Analytics for Firebase and our custom telemetry.
  • Advertising privacy choices: are handled separately through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and control consent for personalized advertising and, where required, storing or accessing advertising information on your device.

Refusing analytics does not block the game and does not prevent non-personalized ads from being shown where they are permitted. Your analytics choice is not treated as consent to personalized advertising, and your advertising choice is not treated as consent to analytics.

6. Withdrawing Consent

  • Analytics: use the switch in Settings → Privacy & Data. Turning analytics off stops future analytics collection and deletes the App's local telemetry log, pending upload queue, and locally generated random installation identifier. It does not automatically erase information already delivered to us or Google; you may contact us to request deletion.
  • Advertising: open Settings → Privacy & Data → Privacy Choices to reopen the available Google UMP privacy options. You may also use applicable Android and Google account advertising controls.

Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

7. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display, deliver, limit, and measure advertisements and to prevent advertising fraud. Depending on your region and UMP choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Google may process the advertising and device data listed in Section 3 under its own terms and privacy policy.

8. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play Billing. Google handles payment credentials through your Google account. We process purchase status and related transaction information as needed to deliver purchased content, restore entitlements, provide support, and prevent fraud.

9. Google Play Games Services

Google Play Games sign-in is optional. If you use it, the current App integration retrieves sign-in status and your Play Games player profile. The game works without signing in. Cloud save, Play Games achievements, and Play Games leaderboards are not currently enabled; this Policy will be updated before those features begin processing additional data.

10. Local Saves and Notifications

Game saves and settings remain on your device unless and until a cloud-save feature is implemented. Uninstalling the App normally removes local App data, but it does not automatically delete analytics, advertising, purchase, support, or platform data already transmitted.

Notifications are generated and scheduled locally on your device. They are optional and, where the operating system requires it, are enabled only after system permission is granted. Notification preferences can be changed in the App and in system settings.

14. Data Retention

  • Custom telemetry: raw events in Cloud Firestore are assigned an automatic expiry time no later than 90 days after receipt. Firestore TTL deletion may occur shortly after that expiry time. Any longer-lived reporting should use aggregated data that does not identify an installation.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: our GA4 property is configured for 14 months of user-level and event-level data retention. This setting does not control all standard aggregated reports maintained by Google Analytics.
  • Local telemetry: retained only while analytics remains enabled and within the App's local size limits; it is deleted when analytics is switched off.
  • Local game saves and settings: retained on the device until deleted through the App, cleared in system settings, or removed with the App, subject to operating-system behavior.
  • Support correspondence: retained only as long as reasonably needed to answer the request and meet legal obligations.
  • Advertising, purchase, and Play Games data: retained by Google according to the settings, legal requirements, and retention rules applicable to each service.
